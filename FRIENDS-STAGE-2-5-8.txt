Zynyro Friends — Stage 2 (items 5–8)

Included:
5. Friends list shows avatar/name, online/offline presence for users currently sharing the Friends presence channel, and per-friend unread counts derived from friend_messages and friend_message_reads.
6. Direct text chat retains separate per-friend conversations, realtime message refresh, sent/received alignment, and timestamps.
7. Read cursors are stored in friend_message_reads and updated when a direct chat is opened. Unread counts are loaded again after refresh. A global Friends-button badge and sound/browser notifications are not included in this stage.
8. Direct-chat voice recording, stop/preview/discard/send, duration/size checks, upload to a private bucket, and playback using signed URLs are included.

Supabase setup:
Apply the new migration:
  supabase db push

Migration: supabase/migrations/20260926160000_friend_chat_messages_presence.sql
It creates friend_message_reads and friend_voice_messages, enables RLS, and creates the private friend-voice-messages storage bucket and policies.

Notes:
- Online status is realtime presence while a user has the Friends panel open; durable last-seen privacy/settings are not implemented yet.
- Voice duration is measured in whole seconds in this UI; uploads are limited to 60 seconds and 350 KiB.
- Existing public and private chat files are retained. No Liquid Glass redesign was made.
- Stage 2 covers plan items 5–8 only; later items remain for subsequent stages.
